# LC Local Project Plan

## Overview

LC Local is a local marketplace and delivery platform designed to connect customers with local businesses, products, services, and independent delivery drivers.

### Primary Tagline

**From the heart of Lewis County to your doorstep.**

## LC Local Platform

The LC Local ecosystem will consist of four connected applications and portals:

### 1. Customer App

Customers will be able to:

- Create an account and manage their profile

- Browse local businesses

- Browse products and services

- Search and filter listings

- Add items to a cart

- Place orders

- Pay securely

- Choose delivery options

- Track orders and deliveries

- View order history

- Save favorite businesses and products

- Receive notifications

- Request Community Care assistance when eligible

### 2. Driver App

Drivers will be able to:

- Create and manage driver accounts

- Complete required driver verification

- View available deliveries

- Accept delivery assignments

- Navigate to pickup locations

- Confirm pickups

- Navigate to customers

- Confirm deliveries

- View delivery history

- Track earnings

### 3. Partner Portal

Local businesses will be able to:

- Create and manage their business profile

- Add and edit products

- Manage pricing

- Manage inventory

- Receive and manage orders

- Update order status

- View sales information

- Manage business hours

- Communicate important information to customers

### 4. Admin Control Center

Administrators will be able to:

- Manage customers

- Manage drivers

- Manage partner businesses

- Manage products and categories

- Manage orders

- Monitor deliveries

- Manage payments

- Manage promotions

- Manage Community Care programs

- View platform activity and reports

- Configure platform settings

## Initial Technology

The planned technology stack includes:

- Flutter for mobile applications

- Firebase for authentication, database, and backend services

- Google Maps for maps, routing, and delivery navigation

- Stripe for payment processing

- GitHub for source-code management

## Core Marketplace Categories

The platform is intended to support categories including:

- Grocery

- Farm products

- Produce

- Pet supplies

- Farm supplies

- Hardware

- Local goods

- Restaurants and food

- Floral products

- Seasonal products

- Local services

Categories will expand as the platform develops.

## Community Care

LC Local will include a Community Care component designed to help people who may have difficulty accessing local goods and services.

Potential features include:

- Sponsored deliveries

- Donated delivery credits

- Community partners

- Assistance requests

- Local business sponsorships

## Delivery Model

The platform will initially focus on Lewis County and surrounding rural communities.

Delivery operations will be designed around scheduled routes, local store pickups, partner businesses, and special delivery requests.
